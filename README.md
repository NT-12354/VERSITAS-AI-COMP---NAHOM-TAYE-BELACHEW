<h2>📖 Overview</h2>

Junior year is the most important year of high school. Grades count the most, standardized tests arrive, and college planning begins, all at the same time. Most students try to manage this with five different apps and a messy Notes file.

Launchpad replaces that mess with one clean dashboard. Open the page and it works immediately. Your information is saved privately in your own browser, so there is nothing to install and no account to create.

<h2>✨ What You Can Do</h2>

| Tool | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 📊 GPA Lab | See your weighted and unweighted GPA update live, and test what-if grades before the semester ends. |
| 🎯 Final Grade Calculator | Find out exactly what score you need on a final exam to reach your goal grade. |
| ⏱️ Focus Timer | Study with 25-minute Pomodoro sessions and build a daily streak. |
| 🎓 College List | Sort schools into Reach, Target, and Safety, check your list balance, and track application deadlines. |
| 🗺️ Roadmap | Follow a 20-step checklist covering the whole year, from fall to summer. |

<h2>🧑‍🎓 Who It's For</h2>

- Juniors who want to stay on top of their grades
- Students preparing for the SAT, ACT, or AP exams
- Anyone starting to build a college list
- Parents and counselors looking for a simple planning tool

<h2>🛠️ Built With</h2>

- HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript
- No frameworks, libraries, or build tools
- Browser localStorage for saving data

<h2>🔒 Privacy</h2>

Launchpad has no server, no accounts, no cookies, and no tracking. Everything you enter stays in your browser on your device. Clearing your browser data will erase your saved progress.

<h2>🎨 Customize It</h2>

Everything is in one file, `index.html`. You can change the grade scale, timer lengths, roadmap tasks, countdown date, and colors by editing the matching section of the code.
